# Audio credits

## Music

All five tracks are dedicated to the public domain under
[CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Credit is not
legally required; it is given with thanks. The licence was checked on each
track's own OpenGameArt page on 16 November 2026.

| File | Used for | Track | Author | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `music_woods.ogg` | Title, the woods, campfires, pages | "Cathedral in the forest (ambient loop)" | congusbongus | https://opengameart.org/content/cathedral-in-the-forest-ambient-loop |
| `music_battle.ogg` | Ordinary fights | "Battle Theme" | Wolfgang_ | https://opengameart.org/content/battle-theme-0 |
| `music_boss.ogg` | Boss fights | "Boss Battle Music" (Epic Boss Battle, seamlessly looping) | Juhani Junkala / SubspaceAudio | https://opengameart.org/content/boss-battle-music |
| `stinger_defeat.ogg` | The death scene | "Medieval: Defeat Theme" | RandomMind | https://opengameart.org/content/medieval-defeat-theme |
| `stinger_victory.ogg` | The escape | "Medieval: Victory Theme" | RandomMind | https://opengameart.org/content/medieval-victory-theme |

`music_woods.ogg` is the author's original OGG file. The other four were
converted to OGG Vorbis (quality 4) from the MP3 or WAV on the source page;
nothing else was changed.

## Sound effects

Every sound effect is generated in code by `sound.py`. No recorded effects are used.
